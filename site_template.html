The Itinerary

How We Roll

  • Everything is optional — your safari, your choice. Pick what suits your interests, fitness and energy.
  • Hub-based travel — each stop offers a menu of day walks, bike rides, kayaking and overnight tramps.
  • Happy Hour HQ — each evening we plan the next day's options and split into groups.
  • Weather rules — plans may change at short notice; we go with the flow.
  • Share the load — taxi-van contribution $4 per person per leg (TBC).
  • Stay connected — logistics, hut bookings and shuttles organised via the WhatsApp group.
