The Itinerary

How We Roll

  • Fifteen hubs, six weeks — the Safari is based around 15 geographical localities, with time to properly explore each one.
  • A flexible trip, not a guided tour — everything is optional. Choose what suits your interests, fitness, energy levels and the weather.
  • A menu at every hub — day walks, occasional bike rides, kayaking and, for those keen, a selection of overnight or multi-day tramps. Choices rather than a fixed schedule.
  • Happy Hour HQ — each evening we talk through the next day's options, decide who wants to do what, organise transport and naturally split into smaller groups.
  • Tramps need logistics — some overnight tramps need vehicle shuttles, drop-offs or van relocations. Start the chat early on WhatsApp and at rallies so hut bookings and transport can be sorted. The tramps are often the easy part!
  • Weather rules — plans may change at short notice. Parking restrictions may spread us over more than one spot in some towns, but WhatsApp keeps us connected.
  • Share the load — a taxi-van contribution of $4 per person, per leg is proposed (TBC).
  • Lay days — may coincide with travel days, or be a chance for a shorter walk, local sightseeing or simply relaxing. Expect the occasional meal out together.
Above all, the Safari is about good company, shared adventure, flexibility and the freedom for everyone to enjoy the journey in their own way.
