How We Roll
Our South Island Safari is based around 15 geographical localities over six weeks; we hope
that gives us enough time to explore each area.

This is a flexible trip, not a guided tour. Everything is optional, and everyone is free to choose
activities that suit their interests, fitness, energy levels and the weather.

Each hub offers a menu of possibilities, including day walks, occasional bike rides,
opportunities for kayaking, and for those keen, a selection of overnight or multi-day tramps.
The programme is designed to provide choices rather than a fixed schedule.

Each evening at Happy Hour we'll discuss the following day's options, decide who wants to
do what, organise transport where needed, and naturally split into smaller groups.

Some overnight tramps may require vehicle shuttles, drop-offs or van relocations. Those
interested should start discussing plans well in advance via our WhatsApp group and/or at
our rallies so hut bookings, transport and other logistics can be organised. The tramps are
often the easy part—the logistics usually take the most planning!

Weather will play a major role in our day-to-day decisions, so plans may change at short
notice. Parking restrictions may also mean we are spread over more than one location in
some towns, but we'll stay connected through WhatsApp.

To help with fuel and transport costs, a taxi-van contribution of $4 per person, per leg is
proposed (TBC).

Lay days may sometimes coincide with travel days between hubs or provide an opportunity
for shorter walks, local sightseeing or simply relaxing. We also expect to enjoy the occasional
meal out together.

Above all, the Safari is about good company, shared adventure, flexibility and the freedom
for everyone to enjoy the journey in their own way.
